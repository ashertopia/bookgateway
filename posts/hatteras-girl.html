Three cheers for Alice!!! This book was AMAZING! It was a good Christian book that included a lot of self discovery and my personal favorite: romance! Hatteras Girl by Alice J. Wisler Bethany House October 2010 This book is amazing! (Have I said that yet?) I got into it incredibly quickly. This book does not contain anything that parents would need to worry about, but I would still recommend it for older kids. What I mean by that is, well, it is a book about a thirty year old going on a bunch of blind dates in order to find true love, and perhaps get a old run down property to own. If you know any kids my age who like that kind of a book, be my guest let them read it, but I do not know anyone other than myself (I'm in middle school) that would like a book like this. Then again I didn't think kids my age would like Twilight and I was wrong there so they might like it. I certainly did:)
