Karla Krae has captured a truly perfect, imperfect love story within this novel. It is a captivating story of two kids with different worlds that battle and struggle for one another. My Once and Future Love Karla Krae Self Published May 2011 Jacob Lindsey and Elizabeth Lawson have a one of a kind love for each other, however, life always seems to get in the way of their happiness. Family, fame, and pride keep them apart for many years but a near death experience gives Jacob Lindsey the wake up call that he needs to see that he truly needs his "Bethie." She is his best friend and one true love, he just hopes that she wakes up so he can begin to make up for all the lost time. Beth is trapped in her own subconscious, in a perfect world because Jacob is there and they are together bound by marriage. It is an addictive story that portrays a realistic relationship. Love isn't story book perfect and Karla Krae has done a phenomenal job creating this story. It is believable and magnetic, once you begin reading the story it is hard to walk away because you can't wait to see what turn or twist is on the next page. This novel takes you from puppy love to love everlasting while invoking the readers attention and emotion through the whole journey. I would definitely recommend this book to others who enjoy a love story. I also suggest An Unlikely Pair which is the prequel to My Once and Future Love because it holds the very beginning to Jacob Lindsey and Elizabeth Lawson.
