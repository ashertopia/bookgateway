Just your average story of three fifth graders who look for a missing little girl in their elementary school and who find themselves in a world of magic and nonsense. Tales of a Fifth-Grade Knight by Douglas Gibson Capstone August 2015 Isaac, Max and Emma's Narnian transition from school play practice to the domain of the Elf King is equal parts fun and silly. Older readers, like me, will find the King's threat of the children getting stuck there to be empty, but the fun isn't in the resolution, but the adventure. All three characters are fun and bring different personalities and perspectives to the adventure. They are joined along their quest by an invisible person, a man-sized bat who is way smarter than he looks, and other zany characters who are slightly different than normal humans (I don't want to give anything away.) This is a fun book that kids in fourth or fifth grade will enjoy. My fourth grader did. He didn't have much trouble understanding words and I was happy to find a clean book without worry about language or activities. We both recommend it.
Reviewed by Scott Asher
