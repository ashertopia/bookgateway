You know you're a Facebook addict when a book about Facebook addiction catches your eye. Yes, you. The Facebook Diet by Gemini Adams Live Consciously May 2013 This hilarious gift book features 50 full color cartoons from talented artist Gemina Adams who uses understated, simple illustrations to point out the truth of modern society's online addictions. The illustrations will have you laughing out loud recognzing yourself in the work! We all know this stuff, but recognizing it is wild fun. Consider some of my favorites, like the one where a man wrote (in paint) on the wall of their friend's house and asked if they saw what he had written on their wall today. Or an illustration that says, "Nothing seems real until you post it..." (Note that some of the art and language, including the one above, while rare, include depictions of nudity, sexual relations, and some language. If that is something you are sensitive to, then you should be aware of it. In total, there are perhaps 5 of the 50 illustrations that could offend.) The book also includes over 20 pages of ideas on how to detox, including facetious ideas, like, "Get sent to jail" to politically charged (and accurate) options, like, "If all else fails... move to China!" An appropriate dessert to the illustrated main course. If you are a Facebook addict or know one this book will make a great gift for those who enjoy art and humor. If Facebook isn't your addiction, Live Consiously also has Twitter, You Tube and Instagram Diet books coming out next year.
Reviewed by Scott Asher
